# Contribute and give feedback

Bitcoin Core App is developed openly by contributors from the Bitcoin Core and Bitcoin Design communities. Testing the preview and sharing your experience is a useful way to help.

## Test the preview

Download the latest build from the [home page]({{ '/' | relative_url }}). A focused test plan will be added later. Until then, feedback about installation, crashes, confusing interactions, and the overall experience is welcome.

## Talk with us

- Join the [#bitcoin-core-app channel on Discord](https://discord.gg/MQzeVTWgaX){:target="_blank"} for discussion and general feedback.
- Follow development or report technical problems in the [bitcoin-core/gui-qml repository](https://github.com/bitcoin-core/gui-qml){:target="_blank"}.
- Discuss design documentation or suggest website improvements in the [BitcoinDesign/Bitcoin-Core-App repository](https://github.com/BitcoinDesign/Bitcoin-Core-App){:target="_blank"}.

Please check existing issues before opening a new one. Include your operating system, hardware, the preview version, and clear steps to reproduce a problem when possible.

## Design collaboration

Design work is collaborative and public. The [Bitcoin Design Community](https://bitcoin.design/){:target="_blank"} hosts the conversation, and project decisions and documentation can be reviewed through the links above.
